# Curate

Curate is a visual workspace for collecting, organizing, and developing ideas.

It gives you a flexible canvas to bring together images, links, notes, and references in one place. Everything can be arranged freely, making it easier to explore ideas, build collections, and keep inspiration organized.

## About

We're building Curate around a simple principle: your workspace should adapt to the way you think, rather than forcing your ideas into rigid structures.

The product is designed to make collecting and organizing visual information feel natural, while keeping the experience fast, focused, and enjoyable to use.

## Status

Curate is currently under active development.

We're working on the core experience and gradually expanding the product with features that make visual organization and creative workflows more useful.

## Privacy

Curate is proprietary software. The source code and internal projects are maintained privately by the Curate team.
